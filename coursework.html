ITC C611 · Analytics Application

Chunk size and overlap for FAISS retrieval: a controlled experiment

Fifteen chunking configurations, each with its own FAISS index, scored on 30 verified questions over a student-organization constitution, with bootstrap confidence intervals and Holm-corrected tests. Chunk size mattered; overlap barely did.

Recall@3 rose from 0.502 at 300 characters to 0.732 at 1,500

A retrieval-augmented chatbot for student-organization governance

Evaluated a RAG chatbot over the JPCS-JRU Constitution and By-Laws on grounding and refusal, across three configurations and five sessions. Removing the fallback directive changed how it refused, not whether it refused.
