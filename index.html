ITC C611 · Analytics Application · 2026

A RAG chatbot that answers from JRU's official bulletin

Ask about a program, a prerequisite or a school policy, and this chatbot answers from JRU's 280-page College General Information bulletin, citing the pages it used. Before choosing the retrieval design, I ran a pre-registered chunking experiment and two follow-up studies. ITC C612 · Analytics Techniques and Tools · 2026

Who files, not what is filed: finding non-compliant building reports

Los Angeles reported a 45-point drop in building energy compliance. After working through 96,213 filings, I found most of that drop came from incomplete paperwork rather than failing buildings, and that the organization filing a report predicts compliance better than the building does. I turned the finding into a model that tells reviewers which filings to check first, served through a Streamlit dashboard.

  • 86.8%
    Of the top 250 flagged filings were non-compliant, vs 7.9% at random
  • 63%
    Of the reported decline came from incomplete filings

ITC C610 · Fundamentals of Analytics Modelling · 2026

Which city employees depend on overtime?

Using 686,091 public payroll records from the City of Los Angeles, I compared four classifiers at spotting employees who rely heavily on overtime. Overtime pay itself was kept out of the inputs so the models couldn't simply read the answer. A second model predicts total pay from six job features.

  • 87.82%
    Accuracy with Random Forest (ROC-AUC 0.9268)
  • 0.9852
    R² when predicting total pay
